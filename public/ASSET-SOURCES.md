# Logo source

`champions-league.svg` is the unmodified official UEFA Champions League logo used by UEFA's website, downloaded on 8 October 2026.

- Asset: https://img.uefa.com/imgml/uefacom/ucl/2024/logos/logo_dark.svg
- Official site: https://www.uefa.com/uefachampionsleague/
- Official logo downloads: https://www.uefa.com/news-media/mediaservices/mediadownloads/
- Copyright and trademark: UEFA. This is not original Pönnicup artwork.

The 180px Apple touch icon and 192px/512px manifest PNGs are raster exports of this SVG using Sharp. The complete logo is scaled proportionally to fit within 68% of the square, centered on midnight blue (#07142f); its paths and lettering are unchanged.
