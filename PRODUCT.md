# Product

## Users and purpose

Pönnicup is the existing five-person Finnish friend group's private Champions League 2026–27 game. Preserve the established coin-based betting, discreet pre-match picks, leaderboard, scorer contest and awards. Make the longer season comfortable to play a round at a time.

The default game starts on 13 October 2026, league-phase matchday 2. Earlier matches are excluded from betting and bonuses. The UEFA standings may include the whole competition.

## The main ritual

Open the current round, scan fixtures grouped by Finnish date, choose 1/X/2, review the shared stake and total, then confirm. The initial quick-bet stake is 20 coins per separate bet. Individual stakes, exact-score bets and scorer bets use a reviewed slip. A selection is not a submitted bet until the server confirms it.

Show the player's liquid bankroll, bankroll-funded open stake and available date-specific bonus pools. Each playing date has its own bonus pool; the pool is shared across that date's matches. Bonus funds are spent first. After the playing date, unused bonus expires unless the liquid bankroll is below the configured recovery threshold, in which case it converts to bankroll. Winnings include stake and return to bankroll. Open bonus principal never raises the ranking.

All match markets use 90 minutes including stoppage time. Two-leg ties track aggregate progression independently. Other players' choices remain private until the match starts. Finnish dates and server time determine display and lock states.

## Data and operations

The game can operate without provider API keys. The admin can add verified fixtures, enter real or group-agreed odds, and submit verified results with reasons. Manual fixture and odds entry live in expandable admin tools. Do not manufacture fixtures, prices, results or leaderboard data to fill the interface.

Automatic ingestion can be enabled later. Provider errors, pending odds and unavailable information remain visible. Manual result corrections are protected from subsequent provider overwrite until the admin explicitly releases the override. Scorer results require separately verified appearance or non-appearance information; unknown participants remain unsettled.

## Trust and inclusion

Keep fixed-user PIN login with a required first-login PIN change. Current PINs accept 6–32 digits. Handle network failures as failures; no production demo sessions or substitute balances. Bet requests carry retry-stable IDs, and server transactions decide the final result.

Support small mobile screens, keyboard input, visible focus, clear status labels, reduced motion and accessible dialog focus. No information relies on color alone.

## Personality

The app is named Pönnicup and covers only the Champions League. Use midnight blue, white and restrained silver throughout the app, with the official UEFA Champions League logo. Use concise, factual Finnish labels and instructions; no marketing slogans, motivational taglines or ornamental copy. Keep compact fixtures. Red communicates missing picks, errors, losses and destructive actions. Preserve clear status labels and accessible contrast. Avoid casino gloss and decorative analytics that do not help the group play.
